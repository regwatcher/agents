- Localization: Paris or London 
- Division: KC Alpha_i   
- Type of contract: Permanent contract  
- Report to the Deputy Chief Executive Officer  

## Main mission 
The Chief Data and AI Officer is responsible for strategy, governance and valuation of data within the group. 

She/He will embody the emergence of a new brand KC Alpha _i, integrating all the commercial and efficiency initiatives leveraging on data and Artificial Intelligence (AI). In that respect, s he/he is a key stakeholder to support KC strategic initiative to embrace new digital technologies. 

She/He leads the "data-driven" transformation of the organization, by ensuring:  
- the quality, reliability and compliance of the data used in the front and middle/back functions,  
- the commercial and analytical valorization of customer and market information,  
- the implementation of proper data governance, 
- the development and deployment of AI use cases within the organization, transforming the group commercial offer and improving the overall productivity. 

## Key Responsibilities  
### 1. Governance and compliance  
- Define and deploy the group's data governance framework (roles, responsibilities, standards, policies).  
- Ensure the quality, consistency and traceability of critical data (customers, instruments, 
orders, executions, interactions).  
- Oversee regulatory compliance of data flows (MiFID II, GDPR, AI European Act, reporting 
obligations). Implement quality indicators and continuous improvement processes.  
- Orchestrate and prioritize the AI initiatives of KC. 
 
### 2. Data architecture and infrastructure 
- Sponsor or support the design and implementation by the relevant technology teams of the 
group data lake/data warehouse , data repository , integrating internal sources (OMS/EMS, 
CRM, research, finance, compliance). 
In that respect, she/he will be responsible for supervising the implementation of one group CRM  within KC and, in close relationship with the CIO, for defining  a simplified target enterprise data architecture, leveraging, if deemed relevant, Snowflake and simplifying the current numerous data  warehouses and referential. 
- Guarantee the interoperability and security of data platforms in conjunction with the IT 
department.
- Define data access standards (APIs, templates, repositories) and support their use by the 
business lines.  
- Coordinate initiatives around master data quality and governance (MDM).  
 
### 3. Data exploitation and valorization Support the front teams (sales  and research) and support functions.  
- Promote AI use cases: customer segmentation, volume forecasting, performance scoring,  
market abuses alert management ...  
- Contribute to the emergence of a KC digital offer (customer portals, data services, external APIs) branded as KC Alpha_i.  

She/he will have especially responsibility to define the pricing of KC digital o fferings, starting with the ones of Trackinsight and based on research data sets. 

### 4. Cross-functional governance and change management  
- Lead a network of data or AI champions in key departments.  
- Manage the relevant data stewardship resources for the data preparation and data scientists’ ones for the AI use cases implementation. 
- Raise awareness among employees of the value and responsibility associated with data.  
- Represent the data function on governance, risk and compliance committees. 

## Relationships 
She/he will interact with all business lines, client management units as well as the Technology department. 

She/he will make sure the proper IT support is built to serve KC Alpha_i ambitions. In that respect, She/He will have to work closely with the group CIO. 

## Desired profile  
Education Engineering degree, business school or university, with a specialization in finance, data  management or information systems. Experience Minimum 10 to 15 years of experience. Proven experience in data governance, architecture, business intelligence and/or digital transformation. Project management skills are a must as well. In-depth knowledge of equity and derivatives flows, execution cycle and MiFID II / GDPR / AI European act constraints.  

## Technical and Behavioral Skills 
Proficiency in data management , AI  and analysis tools. Strong understanding of the front -to-back processes of an equity broker. Transversal leadership, communication skills and ability to influence without hierarchical authority. Strong focus on results and quality of deliverables. Professional English is essential.  

## Indicators of success  
Success of KC Alpha_i offer. Usage of one CRM in the whole organization. Measurable improvement in the quality and reliability of critical data. Reduction of data -related costs (licenses, redundancies, rework). 

Increased satisfaction with customers as well as KC business’ and support functions’ partners.
